Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Lost History Channel ("we", "the Channel") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data, if any, is collected in connection with the Channel's operation and its use of third-party platform APIs.

1. No end-user accounts or personal data collection

Lost History Channel does not operate a public-facing website, mobile app, or account system. We do not collect, store, or process personal data from viewers of our content on YouTube, TikTok, or any other platform.

2. Use of platform APIs

The Channel uses official platform APIs (including the TikTok API and YouTube Data API) exclusively to upload and manage video content that the Channel itself owns. These integrations authenticate solely as the Channel's own account and do not request, access, or store any data belonging to viewers or other third-party users.

3. Analytics

We may review aggregate, anonymized performance metrics made available by platforms (such as view counts or watch time) to understand how our own content performs. This data is provided by the platforms themselves and is not linked to any individual viewer's identity.

4. Third-party platforms

When you watch our content on YouTube or TikTok, your interaction with that platform is governed by that platform's own privacy policy, not by this one.

5. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date above.
